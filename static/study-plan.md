# MIT Science Bowl — 4-Day High-Leverage Plan (2027)

Built from **1,049 questions** across 2023–2025 (Math 341 · Chemistry 356 · Biology 352). Your subjects are **chem/bio/math**, weighted **mostly toward math**. Every topic below is ranked by *frequency × learnability* — points per study hour, not just how common it is. Drill each block in the **Buzz Trainer** tab with the matching subject/topic filter; Science Bowl gives ~5 seconds to buzz, so the goal is **instant setup recognition**, not derivation.

The three math pillars — **Number Theory, Randomness (Prob+Stats), Geometry** — together are **~55% of every math packet**, and two of the three (Randomness, Geometry) are *trending up* year over year. That's your bullseye.

---

## Day 1 — Number Theory (math #1 topic, 60 Qs, dead stable ~20/yr)

Almost entirely **algorithmic** — the best ROI in the whole dataset. The buzz trigger is spotting *which tool* applies. Memorize the formulas to automaticity, then drill.

- **Divisor formulas** — from a prime factorization, divisor-count τ and divisor-sum σ. *(σ(1024)=2047 `2023-r1-t10`; τ(111111)=32 `2025-r9-t15`)*
- **Modular arithmetic + CRT** — "remainder when divided by A, B, C" → Chinese Remainder Theorem. *( `2023-r10-t23`→58, `2025-r4-t21`→138); modular inverses (`2024-r12-t4`)*
- **Legendre's formula** — exponent of prime p in N!; trailing zeros of N! *in any base*. *(`2023-r3-t20`, `2025-r12-t19`)*
- **Base conversion** (2/3/6/7/8/9/16) and base-b palindromes. *(`2023-r1-t14`, `2023-r11-b20`)*
- **Primes & GCD/LCM** — distinct prime factors of N! = primes ≤ N *(`2024-r4-t19`→8)*; gcd·lcm = product *(`2025-r13-b2`)*.

**Drill:** Buzz Trainer → Math, then flashcard any miss and hit **Explain approach**.

## Day 2 — Randomness: Probability + Statistics (62 Qs, *fastest-rising* cluster, 15→18→29)

Probability more than doubled 2023→2025. Highest-momentum area in the data — worth heavy time.

- **Expected value via linearity of expectation** — trigger word "expected"; almost never needs a full distribution. *(11 of 40 prob Qs: `2025-r1-t8`, `2024-r6-t18`, `2025-r13-t2`)*
- **Counting-based probability** — small sample spaces (2 dice, few balls) → just enumerate; watch the **"at least one" → complement** gotcha. *(`2023-r3-t11`, `2025-r5-t9`)*
- **Conditional / Bayes** — "given that…" → P(A∩B)/P(B). *(`2024-r6-b6`, `2025-r8-b4`)*
- **Statistics transformation rules (memorize cold):** multiply data by k → SD ×|k|, variance ×k²; add a constant → shifts mean, not spread. *(`2023-r6-b18`, binomial SD √(npq) `2024-r9-t14`)*
- **"Name the distribution" MC** — pure recall, instant buzz: Poisson, geometric, Cauchy, multinomial. *(`2025-r11-t15`, `2025-r13-t13`, `2025-r14-t11`)*

**Drill:** Buzz Trainer → Math, filter **Type: Multiple choice** for the distribution-ID recall reps.

## Day 3 — Geometry (66 Qs, largest math cluster, rising 18→21→27) + Chemistry fast wins (PM)

### Morning — Geometry (all three flavors), 87% short-answer, all recognition-based
- **Plane:** Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17), special triangles (30-60-90, 45-45-90), **inradius r = Area/semiperimeter**, the 13-14-15 triangle (area 84). *(`2025-r4-t8`, `2023-r9-b12`)*
- **Solid (spiking — 3→3→10 in 2025):** space diagonal √(l²+w²+h²), surface-area/volume of prisms/spheres/cones/pyramids, **Euler's formula V−E+F=2**. *(`2025-r3-t15`, `2025-r13-t21`)*
- **Coordinate:** distance/midpoint, line & circle equations, transformations.

### Afternoon — Chemistry (secondary; pick the *rule/calc* topics that reuse your math brain)
Skip the big-but-deep Organic (76 Qs) and spectroscopy pools — terrible secondary-subject ROI. Instead:
- **Periodic trends** (cleanest rules in chem, feeds the 40 "rank the following three" Qs): IE/EN increase up-and-right; exceptions **B<Be** and **O<N**. *(`2024-r4-t11`, `2025-r4-b5`)*
- **Gas laws** (pure computation — your strength): Graham's rate ∝ 1/√M; ideal-gas; RMS ∝ √(T/M); real gas closest to ideal at **high T, low P**. Know R = 8.314 J/mol·K = 0.0821 L·atm/mol·K, 22.4 L at STP. *(`2025-r5-t5`, `2023-r2-t22`)*
- **Acid/base pH** — recurring "nearest-integer pH" format: strong acid → pH = −log c; know the strong-acid list. *(`2023-r5-t4`, `2023-r5-b4`)*
- **Oxidation-state arithmetic** — fractional averages like Fe₃O₄ → +8/3; just do the math. *(`2023-r3-t10`, `2023-r8-b4`)*

**Drill:** Buzz Trainer → Math (AM), then add Chemistry (PM).

## Day 4 — Math fast-recall + Biology recall decks + mixed timed buzz

### Morning — Math algebra "trick" set (small topics, 100% formulaic, near-free points)
- **Vieta's formulas** — sum/product/reciprocal-of-roots, build a polynomial from roots. *(`2024-r4-b9`, `2023-r4-b17`)*
- **x + 1/x = k → x²+1/x²** (square, subtract 2) — recurs 5×. *(`2023-r6-t11`, `2025-r10-b7`)*
- **Infinite geometric series a/(1−r)** — bouncing balls, fractals. *(`2024-r4-b19`)*
- **Vectors** (100% SA): dot/cross/orthogonality; **|a×b|² = |a|²|b|² − (a·b)²**. *(`2025-r10-t1`)*
- **Log/exponent identities** (11 of 12 are tossups → early-buzz gold). *(`2023-r3-t15`, `2023-r7-t15`)*
- Trig: **cos 2x = 2cos²x − 1**, Pythagorean identities. *(`2023-r1-b10`)*

### Afternoon — Biology (secondary; bio rewards raw recall — closed memorizable decks)
Top 3 bio topics = 60% of the bio pool. Build flashcard decks for:
- **Metabolism + lab-technique mapping** (24%): glycolysis/Krebs/ETC/photosynthesis landmark molecules; **Western=protein, Southern=DNA, Northern=RNA**. *(`2025-r6-t9`, `2024-r14-t17`)*
- **Botany** (16%, *over-weighted vs other contests — under-studied by rivals*): xylem/phloem, ground tissues (collenchyma/sclerenchyma), 5 plant hormones (auxin, **ethylene**…), flower/fruit parts. *(`2024-r3-t20`, `2023-r9-t6`)*
- **Ecology terms** (~15 words cover 34 Qs): r/K selection, survivorship curves, Allee effect, **Batesian vs Müllerian mimicry**, keystone species, nutrient cycles. *(`2024-r1-t19`, `2023-r7-b4`)*
- Quick bonus deck: the **Eye/vision** cluster (recurs every year) and **animal-phyla-by-feature** (nematocyst→Cnidaria, choanocyte→Porifera).

### Evening — Mixed timed buzz (the actual skill)
Buzz Trainer, **all three subjects**, speed cranked up. Practice: (1) 5-second setup recognition, (2) fast mental arithmetic, (3) the **"identify all of the following three"** multi-select format (18 math Qs use it — "1 and 2" / "ALL" / "NONE"). Sanity prior: small integers dominate SA answers (4, 2, 5, 8) — if a tossup yields a huge number, double-check.

### If you only have limited time
Days 1–2 (Number Theory + Randomness) alone cover **~30% of every math packet** and are the most algorithmic. Do those first. Chemistry periodic-trends/gas-laws and Biology ecology/botany decks are the cheapest non-math points if a spare hour appears.
