# 3-Day Cram Plan — maximum points per minute

You compete in **~3 days**. This is not the time to learn new math deeply — it's time to **turn what you know into instant recall** and **memorize the cheap points cold**. Your diagnosis was clear: you're not bad at math, you're *slow* — 11 questions were correct but too slow. Cramming fixes exactly that: reps → recognition → speed.

**The daily rhythm (every day):**
1. **Cram tab** — blitz the day's flip decks until the answers fire instantly (this is the highest-leverage hour you have).
2. **Daily tab** — do your 15 tracked buzzes; watch the streak and your "avg buzz point" drop.
3. **Buzz Trainer** — timed reps on the day's topics; practice buzzing early.

Do NOT chase deep understanding of rare topics. If a card isn't sticking in 20 seconds, move on — you want breadth of the *frequent* stuff.

---

## Day 1 — Math core (your subject, most of your points)
The memorize-cold pack is the single biggest speed win; it converts your 80-second re-derivations into 5-second recalls.

- **Cram → Math**, top of the deck: Vieta signs, $(x-\tfrac1x)^2=(x+\tfrac1x)^2-4$, double-angle $\cos2x=2\cos^2x-1$, $|A\times B|^2=|A|^2|B|^2-(A\cdot B)^2$, variance scales by $k^2$, Pythagorean triples/quads.
- **Number Theory** (biggest, most algorithmic): divisor formulas $\sigma/\tau$, modular arithmetic + CRT, Legendre trailing-zeros, base conversion.
- **Probability**: "at least one" → $1-P(\text{none})$; expected value by **linearity** (never build the distribution); conditional as raw counts.
- Drill: **Buzz Trainer → Math**, then your **Daily** 15.

## Day 2 — Math geometry/algebra + Chemistry's cheap points
- **Cram → Math** (geometry half): special right triangles, inradius $r=\text{Area}/s$, space diagonals (recognize $3\text{-}4\text{-}12\text{-}13$), Euler $V-E+F=2$, geometric series $\tfrac{a}{1-r}$, log/change-of-base.
- **Cram → Chemistry** (fastest non-math points): periodic trends (IE/EN up-and-right, the $B<Be$ and $O<N$ exceptions), gas laws (Graham's, ideal gas, $R$, 22.4 L), nearest-integer pH + the strong-acid list, VSEPR geometry table, fractional oxidation states.
- Drill: **Buzz Trainer** (Math + Chem), **Daily** 15.

## Day 3 — Biology recall + full mixed timing
Bio is pure recall and rewards a cram — closed lists you either know or don't.

- **Cram → Biology**: metabolism landmarks (glycolysis→Krebs→ETC, photosynthesis/RuBisCO), lab-technique → molecule (Western=protein…), botany (xylem/phloem, ground tissues, 5 hormones, flower parts), ecology terms (r/K, survivorship, **Batesian vs Müllerian**, keystone, nutrient cycles), phyla-by-feature.
- **Full mixed timed buzz** (all subjects, Buzz Trainer) — this is the real skill: 5-second recognition. Two rules taped to your monitor: **(1) don't submit until the arithmetic is finished; (2) sanity-check the answer** (small integers dominate).

---

## The night before
Flip **all three Cram decks once**, front-to-back, fast — don't study anything new. Then sleep. Recall is better rested than crammed at 2 a.m.

## If you only have 2 hours total
Cram → Math (the memorize-cold pack + Number Theory) and do 30 buzzes. That alone moves you more than anything else.
